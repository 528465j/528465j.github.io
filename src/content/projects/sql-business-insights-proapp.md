Delivered business insights for ProApp Pvt Ltd through SQL analysis. Used SQL to extract, clean and transform raw datasets, resolving inconsistencies so the downstream analysis was reliable, then applied trend analysis and correlation testing to find patterns in user engagement, revenue and operational bottlenecks.

Brought the findings together in a report with visualisations and prioritised recommendations, and presented them to leadership with a focus on resource allocation and customer retention.
